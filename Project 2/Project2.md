### Let It Grow: Activation and Retention Analysis
This project contains two completed spreadsheet analyses for a B2B collaboration product. Together, they diagnose onboarding friction, define activation moments, prioritize experiments, analyze retention cohorts, and identify levers for improving long-term user value.


### Activation analysis
The activation workbook examines the path from signup to early product value.

Signup-funnel analysis: quantifies drop-off, requested fields, and friction at each signup step.
Experiment design: proposes and prioritizes signup improvements with hypotheses, success metrics, duration, friction addressed, and ICE scores (Impact, Confidence, Ease).
Activation framework: identifies potential setup, aha, and habit moments—the actions that help users reach initial value and repeat behavior.
Moment analysis: measures percentage overlap between candidate activation moments and usage metrics, then visualizes the relationships with line charts.
Activation funnel and segmentation: summarizes the moments that lead to activation and compares activation outcomes by industry, company size, account size, and lead source.
Retention analysis
The retention workbook extends the activation work into durable engagement and revenue health.

Engagement types and states: defines primary and alternate use cases, expected frequency, enabling features, intensity metrics, and casual/core/power-user behavior.
Cohort analysis: tracks active and inactive user retention across weekly cohorts.
Retention curves: visualizes cohort retention over time and compares patterns across segments.
Lifecycle analysis: calculates retained, resurrected, dormant, and weekly active users and visualizes engagement composition.
Churn and LTV: calculates churn rate, retention rate, and lifetime value over a one-year period.
Experiment briefs: proposes activation, engagement, and retention tests with hypotheses, rationale, stakeholders, assumptions, and success metrics.

### Methods used
Funnel-drop-off and friction analysis
ICE prioritization
Setup, aha, and habit moment modeling
Segment analysis
Weekly cohort analysis and retention curves
Lifecycle accounting
Churn, retention-rate, and LTV modeling
Experiment design and measurement planning
Portfolio takeaway
The project demonstrates a full growth-analysis workflow: remove friction before value is reached, identify the behaviors associated with activation, understand which user segments succeed, and use retention and lifecycle evidence to prioritize experiments that reduce churn.
