#### Priceless Penny: SaaS Monetization Strategy
This project builds a 2024 monetization model for Priceless Penny, a B2B online-learning SaaS business with Free, Basic, Professional, and Premium tiers. The work translates acquisition, subscriber movement, revenue, cost, and retention assumptions into a formula-driven Excel model, then turns the findings into a concise business recommendation deck.

### Deliverables
Financial model — formula-driven 2024 customer, revenue, cost, and operating-profit model.
Presentation deck — editable PowerPoint submission.
Presentation PDF — convenient rendered version of the deck.

### Acquisition

Paid-search clicks are calculated as advertising budget divided by cost per click.
The funnel then applies landing-page signup-start and signup-completion rates before distributing new customers across tiers.
A referral loop models invitations sent by active learners, recipient conversion, paid-tier mix, and referral cost.
Tier movement and retention

Upsell, downgrade, and cancellation requests are modelled separately by tier.
Requests made during a month take effect at the following monthly start, which keeps timing consistent with subscription billing.
Growth accounting separates new, returning, churned, and unchanged learners.
Revenue and unit economics

Total revenue is calculated from active paid learners multiplied by plan price.
Expansion, contraction, and churned revenue identify how plan movement changes recurring revenue.
Quick Ratio compares new customers with churned customers.
Net Dollar Retention measures recurring-revenue retention after expansion, contraction, and churn.
Cost of goods sold uses Stripe’s percentage and flat transaction charges. Support, advertising, and referral spending are captured as operating expenses.
Gross profit, gross margin, operating profit, and operating margin are calculated monthly and for the year.

### Recommendations
Reduce the Professional cancellation-start rate from 45% to 35%. Proactive customer-success outreach, annual-plan incentives, and targeted product support can reduce cancellation intent among a high-value tier.
Raise Basic-to-Professional upsell conversion from 40% to 50%. Test clearer Professional-plan value messaging and an upgrade path tailored to the largest paid cohort.
